DEPARTMENT: Patient Safety
DOCUMENT_ID: PS-POL-104
TITLE: Infection Control & Hand Hygiene Protocols

OVERVIEW:
Maintaining strict hygiene standards is vital to preventing Healthcare-Associated Infections (HAIs) across all clinical wards.

CORE PROTOCOLS:
1. Five Moments for Hand Hygiene:
   - Before touching a patient
   - Before clean/aseptic procedures
   - After body fluid exposure risk
   - After touching a patient
   - After touching patient surroundings

2. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
   - Gloves and masks must be worn during contact with mucous membranes, non-intact skin, or bodily fluids.
   - N95 respirators are mandatory for airborne precaution isolation rooms.

3. Incident Reporting:
   - Any near-miss, medication error, or patient fall must be logged into the Safety Incident Portal within 12 hours of the occurrence.
