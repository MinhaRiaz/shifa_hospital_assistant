DEPARTMENT: Admissions
DOCUMENT_ID: ADM-SOP-002
TITLE: Elective Inpatient Admission & Room Allocation

OVERVIEW:
The Admissions Department coordinates room assignments, insurance verification, and pre-admission testing for all planned inpatient stays.

ADMISSION STEPS:
1. Pre-Admission Authorization: Obtain prior authorization from the patient's insurance provider at least 48 hours before planned procedures.
2. Check-In & Identification: Verify patient photo ID and attach an encoded barcode ID wristband to the patient's dominant wrist.
3. Consent Forms: Ensure signed General Consent for Treatment and Financial Agreement forms are scanned into the Electronic Health Record (EHR).
4. Room Allocation: Assign rooms based on clinical necessity (e.g., telemetry, private isolation, or standard ward).
