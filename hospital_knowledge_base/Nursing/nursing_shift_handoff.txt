DEPARTMENT: Nursing
DOCUMENT_ID: NUR-SOP-005
TITLE: Bedside Shift Handover Standard Operating Procedure

OVERVIEW:
Shift handovers must be conducted at the patient's bedside to ensure accuracy, patient engagement, and safety.

HANDOVER METHODOLOGY (SBAR Format):
- Situation: State patient name, bed number, primary physician, and current diagnosis.
- Background: Summarize pertinent medical history, recent surgical interventions, and drug allergies.
- Assessment: Present recent vital signs, pain scores, surgical site assessments, and active IV lines.
- Recommendation: Highlight pending lab results, planned procedures, and critical tasks for the incoming shift.
