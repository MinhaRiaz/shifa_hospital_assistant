DEPARTMENT: Billing
DOCUMENT_ID: FIN-POL-003
TITLE: Patient Billing, Insurance Claims, and Payment Methods

OVERVIEW:
The Patient Financial Services team oversees patient invoicing, insurance claim submissions, and collection processing.

ACCEPTED PAYMENT METHODS:
- Cash and Certified Cashier's Checks
- Major Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- Direct Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) / Online Portal
- Approved Health Insurance / Third-Party Coverage Plans

CLAIM PROCESSING & PATIENT RESPONSIBILITY:
1. Claims are billed directly to primary and secondary insurance providers upon discharge.
2. Itemized statements are issued to patients after insurance adjudication, detailing remaining deductibles or copays.
3. Payment plans are available upon request for balances exceeding $500, payable in up to 12 monthly installments without interest.
